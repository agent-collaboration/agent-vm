B2 · Central organizations: Requirements

An owner creates an organization and runs it through sign-in: it is set up in its own Central store with no deploy, the owner creates and finds workspaces and reads the full history, sees when renewal is stuck, and the organization survives a signing-key change even when idle. Organizations never see each other.

The three things for you

  • Ready means Central confirmed; a lost reply never becomes success.
  • Every workspace and every history record is reachable, however many there are, including the machine records Central already writes.
  • The owner can see an expired pending stamp; an old signing key can't be retired while any organization still depends on it.

Settled 16 Nov: key rotation with no pause (yours); a hidden owner-only maintenance page (yours); the console as its own later feature (mine). Still yours on Decisions: a “yes” to the 24-hour lost-reply recovery, and whether rule 6's Central restart stays.
