B2 · Central organizations: Audit

Most of B2 is already merged. An organization is set up in its own Central store through sign-in, its stamp renews on use, and an owner can create a workspace. Five things keep it from being a finished feature: the workspace list is guessed from the first 100 history records, the history parser rejects four record kinds Central already writes, the browser makes its own operation IDs, the owner can't see renewal trouble, and key retirement has no finished operational path. This page lists what exists, what's missing, and what each piece's evidence really shows.

Back to the Fleet access overview. Switchboard read at f3b2b88b; Central (codex-router) at e7b709f6a. Source observations, not runs.

How renewal works now (kept)

decideOrganizationStampStep is already a pure decision: not-ready, seed, current, resend the one statement in doubt, sent-expired, or sign a replacement. The step-takers then sign, claim the sent slot conditionally, send, and settle with conditional writes that only succeed while the same statement is still held. Every answer to a send that already started is settled, even if the statement expired meanwhile; a stale refusal reads Central's facts and may adopt them even when expired (your R29 change). The existing key-change helper drives the same path, so a ready organization with no local record is seeded from Central by its first pass. B2 keeps all of this and makes the answer handling a closed, pure, exhaustively checked step (Program Design).
