These are different promises. The Lead searched the nominated owner messages and found no first-hand 24-hour decision; “how many ties we retry?” (Fleet transcript line 112302) is a question.

2 · May key retirement pause sign-in?

An old request can already have selected the old key when the operator starts a handover. Re-signing every stored organization does not stop that paused signer from later presenting another old-key replacement. The old public key also verifies already-issued credentials. Safe withdrawal needs either a drained maintenance interval or an explicit live fence across those writers.

The current native key selection is in Better Auth 1.7.11 dist/plugins/jwt/adapter.mjs; the existing B2 re-sign helper has no issuer epoch (organization-key-change.ts:25–68). This is a source-derived race to prove locally, not a claimed observed production incident.

What people see

Sign-in and affected administration pause while issuance drains, old credentials reach their safe expiry, the key is withdrawn and Central is cold-rechecked.

Gain

A bounded operational procedure around the current native key/stamp contracts. No persisted live epoch or new admission format.

Cost and who pays

The owner schedules and runs the window; users wait. Budget roughly 5–10 minutes for a small healthy deployment, unmeasured, subject to the credential inventory and rechecks described below. Failure can extend the pause or abort before withdrawal.
