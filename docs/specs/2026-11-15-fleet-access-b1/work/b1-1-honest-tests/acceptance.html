B1-1 · Honest tests: acceptance

The process I follow to accept B1-1, and the map I fill in as I go. Today every row is "not started". Nothing is accepted from a summary: each row names the file and line it rests on.

The process, step by step

1 · Implementer hands back

File: implementer-handback.md. Passes when it names the PR and head commit, gives gate commands with counts and exit codes, and changed nothing outside the allowed paths.

2 · Verifier observes on the pinned commit

Files: verifier-observation.md and evidence/. Passes when each check passed on both hosts and failed on its wrong build, with the build pin recorded.

3 · Lead check before review

File: lead-check.html. I open the diff and the evidence myself and fill in the map below. Passes when every row has evidence. Any row that can't is not narrowed: it comes to you.

4 · Reviewer, from its fixed packet

File: reviewer-report.html, from the packet. Passes when the verdict is READY and every finding is cited.

5 · Lead acceptance

File: this page, final section. Each reviewer finding is fixed, rejected with evidence, or brought to you. Anything not delivered comes to you before I accept. Then the gate passes, and B1-2 may start once you've given B1 its go-ahead.
