How the reviewer validates, step by step

  1. Answer the first question: list what the change drops against your words, the earlier designs and the old tests.
  2. For each of C1, C1b, C2 and C3, read the design row and your linked words first, and write down what "done" means before opening the code.
  3. Read the code at the pinned commit, not the PR description.
  4. Open the evidence. Check it ran on that commit, on both hosts, and that each check failed on its wrong build.
  5. Any place the brief, the handback or my check narrows, defers or reinterprets a rule is a finding. Never adopt it.
  6. A decision counts as yours only with a link to your words. A relay stays "relayed, unconfirmed".

What it returns, and where

work/b1-1-honest-tests/reviewer-report.html, in the shared format, ending READY or NOT READY. Every finding cites your design row, your words where they apply, and the code line. A finding without them is rejected.
