How the reviewer validates, step by step

  1. Answer the first question: what the change drops against your words, the earlier designs and the old tests.
  2. For each of S1–S6, read the rule and your linked words first, and write down what "done" means before opening the code.
  3. Read the code at the pinned commit, not the PR description. Search the whole of packages/sign-in-core/src/ for any remaining runtime crypto, clock or timer use, including aliasing.
  4. Open the evidence. Check it ran on that commit, on both hosts where required, and that V1–V4 each failed on their wrong build (V5 is a regression check).
  5. Any place the plan, handback or my check narrows, defers or reinterprets a rule is a finding. Never adopt it.
  6. A decision counts as yours only with a link to your words.

What it returns, and where

work/b1-1-host-crypto/reviewer-report.html, in the shared format, ending READY or NOT READY. Every finding cites the rule, your words where they apply, and the code line. A finding without them is rejected.
