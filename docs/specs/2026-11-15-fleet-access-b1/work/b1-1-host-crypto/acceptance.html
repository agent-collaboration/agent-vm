The process, step by step

1 · Implementer hands back

File: implementer-handback.md. Passes when it names the PR and head commit, gives gate commands with counts and exit codes, and changed nothing outside the allowed paths.

2 · Verifier observes on the pinned commit

Files: verifier-observation.md and evidence/. Passes when V1–V4 each passed and each failed on its wrong build, V5 passed on its hosts, and the build pin is recorded.

3 · Lead check before review

File: lead-check.html. I open the diff and the evidence myself and fill in the map below. Passes when every row has evidence. A row that can't is not narrowed: it comes to you.

4 · Reviewer, from its fixed packet

File: reviewer-report.html, from the packet. Passes when the verdict is READY and every finding is cited.

5 · Lead acceptance

File: this page, final section. Each reviewer finding is fixed, rejected with evidence, or brought to you. Anything not delivered comes to you before I accept. Then the gate passes and B1-2 may start.
