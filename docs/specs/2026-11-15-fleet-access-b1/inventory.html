What replaces it

All of this waits on your stop. Nothing below is being built now.

GapReplacementProven by (see Plan)
Passkey sign-inProve that Better Auth 1.7.11 refuses a passkey sign-in without user verification, then turn on the passkey sign-in route and button.Real Chrome with a virtual authenticator, on both hosts: verified passes, unverified is refused.
Membership lookup (R14)The owner-only lookup of a person's membership in the requested organization.Both hosts: the owner gets the answer; every other caller learns nothing.
Organization switching (R5)Keep the choose-organization control on the account page.Real served pages: choose, and the CLI's waiting login returns.
Flood thresholdRun the threshold cases through Miniflare's rate-limit binding.Local workerd with the binding: limited, nothing changed, other address passes.
WatchdogsEvent waits and the controlled clock in the browser harness.Source check: no real timer in tests or helpers.
Page randomnessThe host supplies it; the boundary scan includes every server page file.The scan fails on a planted global call in a page file.
Identifier readback (R32)Read back every stored identifier as UUIDv7.Both databases after a full run.
