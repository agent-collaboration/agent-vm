Decided by you on 15 Nov: click only, no autofill

Your answer: "A. Click only (recommended)", in your review of this page (review 6ab23fa3, document revision 465b72ae). The choice and what each option would have cost are kept below.

What the question was. In Better Auth UI, its PasskeyButton signs in on click. When its passkey plugin's autoFill setting is on (the default), loading the page also asks the browser to list passkeys in its autofill dropdown, in browsers that support it (current Chrome, Edge and Safari). That list appears under a username or email field marked for passkeys. Our sign-in page has no such field: people only choose Google, GitHub or now a passkey.

Every answer keeps Better Auth UI's button unchanged. The choice is one setting on its own plugin: passkeyPlugin({ autoFill: false }) or true. With click only, there's no background request at all. The Turnstile helper still serves overlapping token requests one at a time, so two quick clicks can't share a token (V7).

Key: blue are the starts; rose the autofill cost; violet the missing field; green the click path.

What a person sees

The Continue with Passkey button. No autofill list.

What changes in the code

Better Auth UI's button as shipped, with its plugin set to passkeyPlugin({ autoFill: false }).

What it improves

Loading the sign-in page costs nothing: no Turnstile run, no challenge, no sign-in request counted.

What it costs

Nothing in code. A person with a passkey clicks the button rather than picking from a list, which our page couldn't show anyway.
