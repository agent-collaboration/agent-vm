B1-C · Organizations finished: Audit

Choose any organization you belong to, and, as its owner, look up one person's membership there. The selection exists today only during an unfinished login. The owner lookup is missing. This feature finishes those two paths in Switchboard; the complete getting-in flow stays with BG.

Back to the Fleet access overview. This source audit is against Switchboard f3b2b88b74225359970b0580a290cd34fe1e557b. The Lead approved its scope cut; product work has not started.

The three things for you

  • The chooser stays available after a current organization has been selected. It shows only the signed-in person's own memberships.
  • The owner lookup answers only about the organization requested: member and role, invited, or not a member. A person who exists elsewhere looks the same as someone who doesn't exist.
  • The uncarried-work table gives every missing B1 item a home. BG still owns the setup decision, invitation page, guarded Decline, CLI deadline, account page and six complete local journeys.

Choosing another organization and looking someone up are already approved outcomes. The lookup's page, subject input and organization-choice component are the three open owner choices on Decisions. Neither placement puts administration on the account page; no recommendation is an owner answer.

A refused foreign selection keeps Better Auth's existing behaviour: it clears the current choice. This audit does not promise preservation of that choice or introduce a wrapper that changes it.

Your approved need U9 is to look up “a person's role in your organization (from the sign-in service)” (Requirements, line 106). Your 6 November answer chose “Your account page: your second factors, your organizations (choose one), and a clear ‘done’” (Fleet transcript, line 86867, review 58647d30).

The independent B1 audit calls the chooser partial (R1-O10) and the person lookup missing (R1-O25). I checked their owning code and tests below. These are source observations; no product test, browser journey or hosted service was run for this audit.

Two library behaviours that the design must respect

I used DeepWiki for pointers, then read the installed better-auth 1.7.11 distribution. The indexed description is not the version authority. The local distribution is under switchboard.item2-b2-central-organizations/node_modules/.pnpm/.

Blue names the caller's action; teal the owning check or read; green a successful result; amber Better Auth's refusal effect.

  • Native selection refusal clears the previous selection. dist/plugins/organization/routes/crud-org.mjs:419–433 checks membership; on failure it writes null before throwing 403. The existing foreign-choice test checks the 403 and that no foreign membership was made; it does not assert that an earlier selection survived. B1-C keeps the native effect and must re-read after a refusal. It must not label the selection as unchanged.
  • Native member listing is too broad for this lookup. dist/plugins/organization/routes/crud-members.mjs:437–481 accepts any current member, then returns members and a total. It does not require organization:view. Native get-full-organization similarly checks membership (crud-org.mjs:353–372) and can clear a stale/foreign selection before refusing. Both are closed at the inspected pin. The shipped-component option opens exact native list/full GETs only behind mandatory public summary projection; list-members stays closed. The owner lookup remains a separate live-permission read.

What B1-C hands to BG

A self-view and the native selection operation, not another flow engine. The following is the integration contract developed in the Program Design. It changes no stored schema. BG chooses the page and destination; the sign-in service remains the only authority for membership, session and permission.

What is removed, kept and still undecided

  • Replace the condition that hides organization choice as soon as a valid organization is selected. Extract and reuse the existing choice behaviour rather than maintaining separate B1-C and BG selectors.
  • Keep native set-active, its foreign-choice clearing, sole-membership session initialization, owner/member permissions, first-owner creation, people-change records and fresh-factor gates on changes. Native member-list remains closed; the shipped component option changes only exact native list/full GET offerings behind projection.
  • Add the owner-scoped membership lookup and its real user path. A new read is not a replacement for OD26's change records.
  • BG removes /owner-ready and its unconditional Continue action in its later page cutover (BG parts, line 107). B1-C must leave a reusable choice contract, not another permanent account route that competes with BG.
  • Open: the lookup entry point, exact email versus a picker with fallback, and existing versus shipped organization component; all are compared with costs and answer controls on Decisions. The conditional recommended branch is specified, not selected on the owner's behalf.

The full B1 feature is not accepted by writing these pages. B1-C has seven design pages, still awaits owner answers and independent review, and then needs implementation/proof on both sign-in hosts. BG, B1-B, B2 and B3 remain responsible for their named obligations.
