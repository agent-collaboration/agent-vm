B1-C · Organizations finished: Requirements

A person can see their organizations and switch the current one whenever they like. An owner can ask whether a given person is a member of their organization, invited, or neither. Switching grants no permission, and no answer ever tells anyone about another organization.

The three things for you

  • Switching still works after the first choice, in the same session, and the page then shows what was actually stored.
  • Only the requested organization's current owner can look someone up. A member, another organization's owner, a removed owner or an expired session learns nothing.
  • “Exists in another organization” and “doesn't exist at all” give the owner exactly the same answer: not a member.
  • Reading and switching need no fresh second factor. Inviting, cancelling, accepting and removing keep the protection they have today.

Yours on 15 Nov: the lookup gets its own page in shadcn like Better Auth UI; the owner picks from the organization's list, with email as fallback; the chooser is today's card. Already yours before: the account page shows your organizations with one to choose and carries no admin actions. After a refused switch: Better Auth's behaviour, and the page re-renders so you pick again (your 15 Nov pick, Decisions).

What done means

  • Switching: names and your role in each, the current one marked, switch to another real membership, then the page shows what Better Auth stored. A plain read never switches for you. One membership is still selected automatically at sign-in.
  • Lookup: checked before and after the read; three answers only; an expired, cancelled, rejected or accepted-but-removed invitation is not “invited”.
  • Failure: signed-out or refused reveals nothing; a storage problem shows unavailable; the lookup changes nothing; a new search or a failure clears the old result.
  • Both hosts: local workerd with D1 and local Node with better-sqlite3, through real Chrome.

What BG receives

BG, which owns the getting-in pages, gets facts and an action, not a second idea of membership. It reads readPersonOrganizationContext (signed out, unavailable, or your memberships plus the current choice) and switches through Better Auth's own POST /api/auth/organization/set-active. BG keeps the decision of which page shows next, the return destination, invitations and the CLI deadline. The owner lookup is not part of BG's account page.
