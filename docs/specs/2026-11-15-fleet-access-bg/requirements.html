BG · Getting in: Requirements

After signing in, a person sees one setup step at a time (authenticator app and recovery codes, then their organization) and then goes back to where they were going: a waiting command line, an invitation, or their account page. Every journey is checked on staging after each deploy with no one signing in by hand, and production refuses the test path.

The three things for you

  • The six journeys below are the ones you approved.
  • A command-line sign-in past its deadline goes to the account page with a clear message; it never continues stale.
  • Production can't be entered from staging in any way.
