Admission

The old allowed-email and deployment-owner admission is gone: migration 0001_organization_access.sql:1–7 drops its tables. Admission today is native: the configured owner, members, or a verified email with a pending unexpired invitation (storage/person-session-store.ts:35–86). BG keeps it and removes nothing here.

Native behaviour BG must respect

  • Accept (crud-invites.mjs:264–349) checks the recipient, claims pending → accepted conditionally, creates the member, and rolls back on failure. It is not one atomic write.
  • Reject (crud-invites.mjs:373–406) checks pending and then writes by ID with no condition and no expiry check; it stays closed. BG adds its own Decline that writes only while pending.
  • Get invitation (crud-invites.mjs:498–522) checks the signed-in recipient's verified email before details, so a wrong account learns nothing.
  • testUtils provides helpers, not routes; its login uses internalAdapter.createSession, so our session hooks still run.
