B2X · Person tokens and revocation: Requirements

A person signs in to the CLI by the route you chose, including --device on a machine with no browser, and gets a token that only works with their key and only for what their role allows. When the owner removes someone, changes a role, or a session ends, the owner can see when Central has caught up, and nothing is called “revoked everywhere” before it has.

The three things for you

  • --device works and the token still dies with its session.
  • The owner sees “waiting for Central” after a removal, then “reached Central” only after Central's acknowledgement.
  • Every counterexample in the r3 design's proof list has an owner and a planned check.
