The split with Lead A

Lead A (push 01a1e164-ea38): B3 depends on 4a, “Routers write to Central over the link”, not 4b. What 4a delivers is built on C2 (963f5739d) and keeps its shape:

  • Machine token check: compact JWT, EdDSA, typ at+jwt, nonempty kid, verified against the sign-in issuer's JWKS; exact audience; client_id == azp; no workspace_id claim; expiry min(exp + 60 s, grant_expires_at), refused at grant_expires_at (collaboration-central-core/src/machine_token.rs:163–176).
  • Door: GET /v1/organizations/{organization}/link, bearer machine token, WebSocket (collaboration-central-tokio/src/workspace_link.rs:52; Worker workspace_link_entry.rs). 401 on a refused token, 503 on admission failure.
  • Admission: the installation resolves to its machine row, which must be in that organization, enrolled, with the active enrollment and matching client; every write re-reads authority and allowed workspaces (machine_connection_admission.rs:12).
  • Renewal on the open connection: 4a adds RenewMachineToken, as you approved (R40), replacing C2's new-socket renewal (Lead A, push 01a1e16a-1dc3).

Which of the enrollment, allowance and command pieces at Central are 4a's and which are B3's is being confirmed with Lead A (Decisions, my calls).
