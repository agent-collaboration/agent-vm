B3 · Machine login: Requirements

A Router logs in by device code: you approve it on the sign-in page and choose its workspaces. It gets short-lived machine tokens, connects to Central only through the sign-in service, and renews on the open connection with no one acting. Revoking it refuses it at once and closes its connection.

The three things for you

  • Only you approve a machine, and it belongs to one organization.
  • A code works once; two Routers you approved stay independent.
  • Revoke closes the connection and refuses the next renewal.
